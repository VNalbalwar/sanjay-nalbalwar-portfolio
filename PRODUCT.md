# Product

## Register
Experience — a digital archive and legacy platform; the work leads, the interface recedes.

## Subject
Prof. Dr. Sanjay Laxmikant Nalbalwar — Professor Emeritus, Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Technological University (DBATU), Lonere. 36 years teaching, 20 research, 16 administrative. Field: signal & image processing, compressed sensing, multirate filter banks, biomedical signals, antennas, wireless, now quantum technologies. Institutional leader (Dean Academics, IQAC, DFIIE, NEP-2020, Marathi technical-book translation).

## Audience
University administrators, professors, Ph.D. scholars, policymakers, publishers, conference organisers, recruiters. They scan for verifiable credibility: metrics with sources, patents with true status, roles with dates.

## Job of the site
Let a visitor understand a whole career in minutes and verify any claim in one click. Remains useful for decades, after the administrative tenure has ended.

## Tone
Distinguished, academic, timeless, modern. Formal and plain. No superlatives, no invented quotes or opinions, no inflated numbers.

## Hard rules
- Everything is about Dr. Nalbalwar only. No developer credit, no other person's details.
- Never fabricate publications, awards, patents, roles. Never call an application a grant.
- Metrics always carry source and retrieval date; sources are shown side by side, never merged.
- No phone number. Email only as published in the biodata.
- Raw biodata PDF is private; never published (it holds referees' phone numbers).
- Unverified items are visibly marked "awaiting confirmation".
- Free tier only.

## Anti-references
Developer-portfolio card grids, SaaS gradient heroes, cream + terracotta, eyebrow labels, hero-metric template.
